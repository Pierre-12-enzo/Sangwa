Sangwa Polyclinic is a specialised maternity, paediatric, and internal medicine practice serving families across the Southern Province. Founded in late 2023 near CHUB in Huye, we combine international standards of care with the warmth of Rwandan hospitality — so you don't have to travel to Kigali for world-class treatment.
